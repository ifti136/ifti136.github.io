I'm a final-year Computer Science and Engineering student at Daffodil International University (CGPA 3.71/4.00), and Vice President of the Research Wing at the DIU NLP & ML Research Lab. My work spans machine learning research — from IoT intrusion/malware detection to medical image classification — and practical software engineering, including a MiniJava compiler with an x86 backend and a Flutter inventory-tracking app used by a real small business. I enjoy breaking down complex problems and turning them into working, efficient systems.
